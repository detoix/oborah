# Taxonomy of Formats

All apps in this portfolio fall into one of five formats. Each format uses the same underlying engine but differs in the interaction model — what the user does with the 3D scene.

## Format 1 — Configurator

**Pattern:** User enters address → sees their real environment → designs something by placing, arranging, and customizing objects in that space.

**Interaction:** Drag and drop, resize, snap to grid, material picker, component catalog.

**Monetization angle:** High commercial intent. The user is planning a purchase. Ads from material suppliers, home improvement stores, and service providers are highly relevant and high-CPC.

**Engine mode:** `design`

## Format 2 — Visualizer

**Pattern:** User enters address → sees their real environment → toggles options to see how something would look on their property. No drag-and-drop — just selection and preview.

**Interaction:** Choose from options, toggle on/off, slider controls. Simpler than configurators.

**Monetization angle:** Same high commercial intent as configurators. The user is evaluating a purchase or improvement. Relevant ads are high-CPC.

**Engine mode:** `visualize`

## Format 3 — Calculator / Analyzer

**Pattern:** User enters address → the app computes and visualizes something about their location. No object placement, no design. Pure analysis overlaid on the real environment.

**Interaction:** Minimal — enter address, maybe adjust parameters (date, time, thresholds). The output is the visualization itself.

**Monetization angle:** Very high-CPC ads. Solar companies, insurance, real estate, construction services all bid heavily on users who are actively analyzing their property.

**Engine mode:** `analyze`

## Format 4 — Explorer

**Pattern:** User enters address or browses a map → discovers data about their area visualized in 3D. Not about their property specifically but about their neighborhood, city, or surroundings.

**Interaction:** Pan, zoom, filter, toggle data layers. More like Google Earth than a design tool.

**Monetization angle:** High traffic volume, moderate CPC. Real estate, moving services, insurance, local services. The engagement time is high which helps ad impressions.

**Engine mode:** `explore`

## Format 5 — Game / Toy

**Pattern:** User enters address → plays with a physics-based or whimsical 3D experience on their real environment. Not useful. Pure engagement and shareability.

**Interaction:** Varies — physics simulation, object dropping, parameter tweaking. Fun-first.

**Monetization angle:** Low CPC but very high traffic potential. Viral shareability funds the ad volume. These are also cross-promotion vehicles for the useful tools.

**Engine mode:** `play`

## Tagging and cross-referencing

Each idea should reference:

- **Mode**: ground-plane, wall, room, map-only
- **Map integration**: required, optional, none
- **Asset complexity**: procedural-only, procedural+textures, needs-hero-models
- **Estimated CPC tier**: high, medium, low
- **Estimated search volume**: check with keyword tools before building
- **Related apps**: which other apps in the portfolio cross-promote well with this one
