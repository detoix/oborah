# 3D × Real World — Project Overview

## What is this?

A portfolio of hundreds of small, independent, ad-monetized web apps. Each app provides an interactive 3D experience grounded in real-world location data. Users enter an address and interact with a 3D scene overlaid on their actual environment — their yard, their roof, their street, their neighborhood.

The current example app in this repo is a vegetable garden planner: a map-grounded layout tool for arranging beds, planters, and related garden elements in a real space.

## Why does this work?

Three converging truths:

1. **AI killed text-based affiliate content.** Every "top 10 drills" article can be generated by ChatGPT. Google is replacing it with AI overviews. Static content is a dead end.
2. **AI cannot do interactive 3D.** Spatial, visual, manipulable experiences are the last mile of decision-making that no LLM can replicate. A chat interface doesn't help you see if walnut or oak looks better on your deck.
3. **Real-world grounding transforms toys into tools.** "Design a deck" is a toy. "Design a deck on your actual backyard pulled from satellite data" is something a landscape architect charges €500 for.

## The cattle model

No single app matters. The portfolio earns. Each app is deployed, indexed by Google, and left to accumulate organic traffic. Some will rank. Some won't. The marginal cost of adding a new app is near zero once the engine is built.

This is the 2025 equivalent of the old "200 city pages with ads" model — but instead of scraped text content, each page is an interactive 3D tool that no AI can generate and no competitor will bother replicating at this scale.

## Five formats, one engine

All formats share the same technical core: address input → map/satellite data → 3D scene → interaction → ads.

| Format | What the user does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Configurator** | Designs something in a space | Design your deck on your yard |
| **Visualizer** | Sees something overlaid on their location | See solar panels on your roof |
| **Calculator** | Gets analysis of their location | How much sun does your garden get |
| **Explorer** | Discovers data about their area | Construction projects near me |
| **Game** | Plays with their real environment | Drop a marble on your street |

## Constraints

- The creator cannot publicly advertise this under their real name
- No LinkedIn, no personal brand, no face on camera
- All promotion happens under brand accounts or through organic SEO
- Distribution is programmatic, not manual
- The business is quasi-passive: build, deploy, check monthly

## Tech stack summary

- Next.js (App Router) + React 19
- MapLibre + PMTiles + Protomaps basemaps
- Photon geocoding (current vegetable garden planner integration)
- AdSense (monetization)
- Obsidian (this vault — backlog, docs, tracking)
- NPM workspaces (monorepo management)
