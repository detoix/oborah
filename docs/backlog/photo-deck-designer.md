## Notes

- **Core Loop**: User takes a picture of their house facade / garden. The app projects this image onto a flat 2D plane in the 3D scene (acting as the background wall).
- **Interaction**: The user can then draw a deck, terrace, or pergola _perpendicular_ to that photo plane.
- **Value Prop**: Bridging the gap between a purely top-down map view and a blank slate. By using their actual photo, users get immediate, highly realistic, contextual visualization of how a deck will look attached to their actual home.
- **Technical Challenge**: Needs a way to define the scale/perspective of the photo so the 3D deck aligns correctly (e.g., "draw a line on the photo that is 2 meters long").

## Assets needed

- [ ] Procedural deck planner logic (extruding shapes)
- [ ] Photo projection plane component (supporting image upload and scaling calibration)
- [ ] Procedural railing systems
- [ ] Procedural pergola generator
- [ ] Basic outdoor props (chairs, tables, BBQ) for scale context

## SEO observations

- Searching for "design deck on my house photo" or similar terms represents users who have visual ideas but lack drafting skills.
- High buyer intent; materials (composite, wood, fasteners) are expensive, making these high-value AdSense targets.
