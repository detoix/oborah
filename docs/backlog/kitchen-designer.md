## Notes

- **Core Loop**: Users draw walls, place windows and doors, and arrange furniture and appliances.
- **Customization**: Ability to change furniture handles, cabinet fronts, and countertop materials.
- **Minigame / Simulation**: Crucial differentiator. After designing, users must complete a list of tasks in their 3D kitchen (e.g., making coffee, cooking dinner).
- **Goal**: This gamified simulation helps users find out if their design is functionally good (e.g., testing the "kitchen work triangle", checking if the fridge is too far from the stove).
- This approach turns a standard configurator into an interactive game, significantly boosting dwell time and ad revenue potential while providing real utility.

## Assets needed

- [ ] Walls, windows, and doors (procedural)
- [ ] Kitchen cabinets: base, wall, and tall variants (procedural with customizable fronts)
- [ ] Hardware catalog for cabinet handles (GLB)
- [ ] Major appliances: fridge, oven, stove, dishwasher, sink (GLB)
- [ ] Task items for minigame: coffee maker, pots, pans, plates, food items (GLB)
- [ ] Avatar or first-person controller logic for the minigame interactions

## SEO observations

- Kitchen planning is a high-cost commercial intent category, leading to very high CPCs (retailers, custom cabinet makers, appliance brands).
- Adding the "simulation/game" angle can capture long-tail keywords that standard configurators miss.
